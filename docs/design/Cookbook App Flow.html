The loop closes
Cooking a recipe decrements the pantry. Pantry feeds the shopping list. Shopping list feeds next week's plan. Notes ("14 min, not 12") show up the next time you make this. The app gets sharper every week.
★

Four things this app commits to.

The non-negotiables. Every feature in V1 serves at least one of these.

i.
Amounts live inline
In every step, in every recipe, no matter the source. Scrolling back is a bug.
ii.
One source of truth: pantry
Recipes know what you have. Shop knows what you need. Pantry updates when you cook.
iii.
Plan once, shop once
Sunday gives you the whole week. The shopping list is grouped by aisle, ready to send.
iv.
Import is one tap
⌘V, share sheet, Chrome extension. Anywhere you find a recipe, it's already saved.
